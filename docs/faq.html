What Xenon is

What is Xenon?

A free dashboard for the screen next to your PC. It shows your system (CPU, GPU, temperatures, network), what is playing, your calendar, the weather and your notes, and it has the Deck: keys you tap to open apps, control music or run shortcuts on the PC. You arrange it with widgets and themes. It runs on Windows, with macOS and Linux in beta.

Do I need a Xeneon Edge or a CORSAIR PC?

No. Xenon was designed around the CORSAIR Xeneon Edge, but it runs on any PC and needs no CORSAIR hardware or software. iCUE only matters if you want the dashboard inside an iCUE widget, or to drive CORSAIR RGB devices.

Which screen can I use?

Any screen your PC can show a window on: a spare monitor, a touchscreen, or a window on your main display. The app asks which screen to open on the first time it runs. A tablet or a phone works too, over your own Wi-Fi: it shows your real dashboard with the same layout, widgets and theme, and its keys really run on the PC. The phone guide and the tablet guide show how.

Does it work on Mac and Linux?

Yes, in beta: macOS 11 or later (one download for Apple Silicon and Intel) and 64-bit Linux, next to Windows 10 and 11. The dashboard, the widgets and the Store are the same on all three. Some sensors and system actions are still missing on macOS and Linux, and a tile with no data on your system says so instead of loading forever. The Mac and Linux pages list what works.

First launch on a Mac: the app is not notarized yet, so macOS blocks it the first time. On macOS 15 and later open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. On macOS 11 to 14, right-click the app, choose Open, then Open again. You do it once per version.

Is it free? What do supporters get?

The app is free, with no ads and no account. Supporters on Buy Me a Coffee get the monthly drops in the catalog, the Lyrics and Developer cleanup widgets, a role on the Discord and a place in the Hall of Supporters. It is 5 USD a month or 50 USD a year, or a one-time amount from 5 USD, where every 5 USD counts as a month. The code arrives by email and goes in Settings → Support Xenon. Every plan also brings 300 Sparks a month, Xenon's credits.

You can try the supporter pass free for 7 days, once per PC: it opens the supporter drops and Lyrics. Limited drops, and some new ones in their first weeks, are for supporters only. Lyrics and Developer cleanup stay unlocked after your support ends. A year of support also gets you a theme made for you from your palette or a photo, published with your name on it.

What are Sparks?

Sparks are Xenon's credits: 1 Spark is 1 US cent. You spend them on the assistant when you use it without an API key of your own, and on the Store items sold for Sparks. Every month of support includes 300, which reset at the start of each month. You do not need to be a supporter to buy more: they are extras on Buy Me a Coffee, 500 Sparks for 5 USD, 1050 for 10 USD or 2200 for 20 USD, and bought Sparks never expire. If you are not a supporter, a Sparks code (XC-…) arrives by email with your first pack: it goes in the same box as the supporter code.

An item bought with Sparks is yours for good, updates included, on up to 3 PCs, and installing it again costs nothing. Limited editions are never sold for Sparks.

Using it

Does it slow down my games?

It is built not to. While a game is running, Game mode fades out the animated background and pauses animated effects, and the heaviest tiles, the Browser and Second screen streams, stop until you finish. Live tiles like those and Home Assistant only stay connected while they are on screen.

On Windows, touching the dashboard in the app mid-game is safe: the cursor goes straight back to the game's monitor and the tap does not take the game's focus.

How do I add widgets and themes?

Tap + on the dashboard to add a built-in tile. For more, open Settings → Store: themes, backgrounds, widgets, ambient scenes and packs made by the community, each with a preview before anything is applied, installed with one tap. You can browse the same things in the catalog on this site.

Can it show temperatures and in-game FPS?

Yes. On Windows, the installer sets up temperature reading (it needs the administrator prompt once), and PresentMon gives the real frame rate of the running game, fullscreen titles included; Settings → Game mode installs it if it is missing. On macOS temperatures and GPU load come from macmon (brew install vladkens/tap/macmon). On Linux, in-game FPS needs MangoHud, with the game launched through it.

Does it work with Spotify, Home Assistant and RGB lights?

Yes, each one optional and off until you connect it.

  • Spotify: whatever you play shows in the Media tile. Link your account in Settings to add seek, like, shuffle and playlists, also from the Deck (playback control needs Spotify Premium).
  • Home Assistant: connect it in Settings → Smart Home with your address and a long-lived token, then control lights, sensors and scenes from a tile or a Deck key.
  • RGB: CORSAIR devices through iCUE, plus WLED, OpenRGB, Philips Hue, Nanoleaf, Govee, LIFX and Yeelight over your network, and GIGABYTE and AORUS motherboards over USB on Windows.
Can I save my layout or move it to another PC?

Yes. Settings → Backup → Export backup saves one file with your layout, Deck, calendar, tasks, timers, notes, settings and custom background. On the other PC, Import backup puts it all back. API keys and passwords are never in the file, so integrations ask you to connect again, and community widgets are listed so you can reinstall them from their codes.

When something goes wrong

Windows says Xenon is a virus. Is it safe?

It is a false positive. The Windows installer has been signed since version 4.11.9, but Microsoft builds trust in a new file over time, so Defender, SmartScreen or your browser can still warn about a fresh release. You can check the file yourself: every release ships a signed SHA256SUMS list, and the download page shows the one command to compare it. You can also install Xenon from the Microsoft Store.

  • Defender removed it: open Windows Security → Virus & threat protection → Protection history, pick the Xenon entry and choose Restore. Then add %LOCALAPPDATA%\Xenon and %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Xenon under Exclusions.
  • The browser blocks the download: open the downloads list with Ctrl+J and choose Keep.
  • It does not start and nothing was reported: on Windows 11 that is usually Smart App Control, under Windows Security → App & browser control. Try the latest release with it still on first: once you turn it off, Windows does not let you turn it back on without resetting the PC.

Only bypass a warning for a file from the official Releases page whose hash you checked.

Xenon closes on its own

Open the tray menu and choose Open crash log. If the last line is a PANIC, the app failed by itself: paste that line on the Discord or in a bug report, it names the exact spot. If the last line is launched with nothing after it, something outside the app closed it, almost always the antivirus: look in Protection history for an entry at that moment, restore it and add the two exclusions from the question above.

I can’t see the CPU temperature

On Windows, run the installer again and accept the administrator prompt: reading temperatures needs LibreHardwareMonitor and PawnIO, which include a driver-level part, and that is what the prompt is for. Decline it and everything else still works, just without temperatures. On macOS, install macmon. Some laptops and boards expose no sensor at all; the tile then says so instead of showing an invented number.

How do I update or uninstall it?

Xenon updates itself: when a release is out the dashboard asks, one tap downloads it, checks its signature before installing, and puts the previous version back if anything fails. Your settings, layouts and notes stay.

To uninstall, run UNINSTALL.bat on Windows, UNINSTALL.command on macOS or ./UNINSTALL.sh on Linux. It removes the app, the startup entry and your data; add -KeepData on Windows or --keep-data on the others to keep your settings, layouts and notes.

Privacy and help

Does it send my data anywhere? Do I need an account?

Your settings, notes, tasks, events, Deck and file index stay on your PC. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporter and no behavioural tracking in the app. It does go online for some things: checking for updates, and the services you turn on yourself, like the weather or Spotify. The privacy page lists every call it can make and what it sends.

There is no Xenon account. A supporter code is the only thing you ever enter, and integrations use their own logins.

Where do I get help?

Ask on the Discord: it is the fastest, and questions that keep coming back end up on this page. For a bug you can repeat, open a GitHub issue with your system, what you did and what happened.

Personal use is free, including on the PC you work on. Selling Xenon or shipping it inside a commercial product needs written permission first: email me.
