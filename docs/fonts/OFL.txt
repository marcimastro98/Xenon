Subsets are Google Fonts' own latin and latin-ext ranges, taken unmodified. The
unicode-range declarations that go with them live in ../site.css; changing a
subset means changing both or a glyph silently stops resolving.

Nothing here is loaded from a third party at runtime. That is deliberate on a
site whose claim is that things stay on your machine: a webfont request tells
another company which page a visitor opened, and there is no reason for it.
