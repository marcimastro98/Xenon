Xenon, widgets for your PC on any screen

Xenon runs on your PC (Windows, with macOS and Linux in beta) and shows on the screens around it: a spare monitor, a touchscreen, a tablet or your phone. This page opens share codes for themes, pages, Deck profiles and widgets, usually from the QR code on a share card.

Free, no account

  1. Copy the code on your PC, or send this link to yourself.
  2. In Xenon, open Settings → Widgets & sharing → Share & Import → Import.
  3. Paste it, check the preview, then Apply.
