No second screen? Beta

You already have one in your pocket.

Xenon opens in your phone's or tablet's browser, and it is the same dashboard you have on the PC: the same widgets, the same theme. Press a Deck key and it fires on the PC.

See how it connects
  • Thirty seconds Switch it on at the PC, point your phone at the QR code. Done.
  • Nothing to install No app and no account. It opens in the browser you already have.
  • It never leaves your home Phone and PC talk over your own network. No cloud in between.
01The essentials

Start with
these five.

There are 40 widgets in the palette. These five are the ones the rest is built around.

01Xenon AI

Ask it out loud

Say “Hey Xenon” and it answers. Change the track, read out your day, or have it build you a whole dashboard page. Use the AI you already pay for (Gemini, ChatGPT or Claude), or a free local stack (Ollama, Whisper, neural voices) that never touches the internet.

Voice + vision “Hey Xenon” wake word Local or cloud
02Deck & streaming

Every shortcut on one grid

A grid of programmable keys inside the dashboard. Launch apps, switch OBS scenes, go live on Twitch, fire hotkeys and webhooks, control Discord voice and Spotify. Folders, multi-step macros and per-key RGB are included.

OBS · Twitch · YouTube Discord voice Macros & webhooks
03Monitoring

CPU, GPU, RAM and FPS, live

Live charts for your sensors, your network and your real in-game FPS, with 30 days of history so you can see what changed. Sensors are read on your PC. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Live charts In-game FPS 30-day history
04Search

Pull the bar down and type

Pull the top bar down and the search opens. Type the way you talk: “photos from december”, “big videos from this year”. What Xenon understood shows up as tags you can switch off. The list of files lives on your PC and is never uploaded. There is an AI mode too, and all it ever sees is your sentence.

Type it like you say it Gemini, Claude, OpenAI or local Ollama One pull to open
05Disk space

No scan button, ever

Tap a drive and the map is already there. No waiting, no scan. Tap a folder to go inside it, look at your biggest files, and find the duplicates you forgot you had. When it comes to cleaning up, Xenon only ever offers things you can afford to lose: temporary files, browser caches, old installers. You confirm each one, it all goes to the Recycle Bin, and Xenon AI can look at the numbers but cannot delete anything.

Always up to date Everything goes to the Recycle Bin The AI cannot delete
02The phone, at work

Your phone stops being a separate island

Files go both ways: pick photos on the phone and they land in Download/Xenon on the PC with their own names, or drag a file onto the tile and download it there. A countdown you set on the dashboard reaches the phone with its screen off. And when someone calls you on Discord, Teams or the phone itself, the dashboard takes the screen and tells you who it is.

03The companion

Bit, the pixel guardian

Vitals tracks hydration, focus and posture like a game HUD. Bit is the pixel pet in the corner who notices when they drop, and keeps bothering you until you take the break. It can be sarcastic. It is never mean. It stays off until you turn it on.

"Signed: your guilt, in 8-bit. — Bit"
04For the glow

RGB that reacts to real data

Philips Hue, WLED, Govee, LIFX, Nanoleaf and your CORSAIR gear, driven by CPU temperature, album art or a timer running out. It runs alongside iCUE without fighting it, and it stays off until you switch it on.

08For creators

Make Xenon yours.
Then share it.

A theme is a palette. A widget is a folder with an HTML file in it. The guide covers both, start to finish. No build tools, no account, and you can ask the AI for help while you work.

01 Pick what to create

A theme, an animated background, a custom widget, an ambient scene, or a pack that bundles them together.

02 Follow the guide

Copy-paste examples for every kind of content, including Dynamic Island Live Activities, full-bar activities and takeover events.

03 Publish it

Export it as one short code. Trade it on Discord, or submit it to the catalog with your name on it.

macOS and Linux are in beta. The dashboard, the widgets, the Store and the integrations are the same everywhere; what still differs is the part that reads your machine, and the README table says exactly what works where.

Windows blocked the download, or called it a virus? That is a false positive: the build is not code-signed yet, so it starts from zero reputation. The FAQ explains how to check the file's hash yourself, and how to get it back.
